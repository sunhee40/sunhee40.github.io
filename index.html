Current engagement

Implementing a market-data platform that feeds OMS, IBOR, ABOR, and downstream systems for a global asset servicer

As a Senior Consultant at Citisoft, I work on the market-data layer supporting a global asset servicer's investment operating environment—the flows that supply trading, IBOR, ABOR, collateral management, and reconciliation platforms.

When downstream systems disagree, the investigation comes back to the data: where it originated, how it was configured, how it moved, and where it changed. It is a systems problem before it is a finance problem—many sources, many consuming platforms, and no tolerance for a number that cannot be explained.

  • Led implementation through every gate — moved data through configuration, business and client acceptance testing, production parallel, and production to a successful go-live.
  • Mapped the operating environment — documented end-to-end global data cycles across the order management, IBOR, accounting, and downstream platforms consuming the data.
  • Root-caused defects with SQL — investigated anomalies across multiple Snowflake schemas and traced security-master and account-configuration failures to their source.
  • Reconciled complete position populations — verified data integrity across conversion accounts rather than relying on samples.
  • Designed the testing approach — developed analytical workflows across asset classes to identify discrepancies during testing instead of after production release.
